- **Service dimensions (all int 0–5)**: `EntertainmentRating`, `FoodRating`, `GroundServiceRating`, `SeatComfortRating`, `ServiceRating`, `ValueRating`, `WifiRating`. Value `0` is extremely frequent (e.g. WifiRating=0 in 99,978 rows / 77%; Entertainment=0 in 54,120) and almost certainly means **not rated / not applicable**, NOT a true zero score. - **Dates**: `DateFlown` (e.g. "November 2019"; 112 distinct months; 29.71% missing) and `DatePub` (e.g. "11th November 2019"; 5,200 distinct; 0 missing). Both are strings needing parsing; ordinal suffixes (11th/25th/31st) present.

## 5. Data-quality issues (documented, not yet fixed)

1. `0` in 0–5 service ratings conflates missing with scored — recode to NaN with documentation.
2. High missingness: Aircraft 71.9%, TripVerified 54.0%, Route 29.8%, DateFlown 29.7%, TravelType 29.6%.
3. Malformed `TripVerified` categories (see above).
4. `OverallScore` missing 3.35%; `Review` empty 0.64%.
5. Class imbalance: Recommended 60/40; OverallScore dominated by 1-star (34%).
6. No verified license/source URL for the CSV — recorded as NOT YET VERIFIED in README/report later.

## 6. Potential targets and leakage

- **Candidate targets**: `Recommended` (cleanest, binary, no missing); `OverallScore` banded or proxy sentiment derived from it (must be labelled as proxy, and the source rating must then be excluded from features); service-dimension ratings (weaker, hurt by 0-as-missing).
- **No explicit human-annotated sentiment column exists** — sentiment must be derived transparently or modelled via `Recommended` as the business outcome.
- **Leakage risks**: (a) predicting `Recommended` with `OverallScore` (or vice versa) — near-duplicate outcomes; (b) predicting proxy sentiment derived from `OverallScore` while feeding `OverallScore`/service ratings as features; (c) `Title` paraphrases `Review`; (d) `Slug` duplicates `AirlineName`; (e) `DatePub` occurs after the flight; (f) `unique_id` is a pure key.

## 7. Recommended ML/NLP tasks (based on actual data)

1. **Primary**: predict `Recommended` (yes/no) from `Review` text + cabin/travel/airline + service ratings (with 0→NaN handling). Evaluate with precision/recall/F1 + confusion matrix, not accuracy alone (60/40 imbalance).
2. **Secondary**: 3-class proxy sentiment from `OverallScore` (e.g. 1–3 negative / 4–6 neutral / 7–10 positive — thresholds to be justified in Phase 4 after inspecting scale use), text-only features, rating excluded from inputs.
3. **Risk indicator**: Customer Experience Risk Score from negative prediction + low OverallScore + multiple low service dimensions + `Recommended=no` — only as a documented analytical indicator.
4. **Topics**: TF-IDF n-grams/LDA over `Review` for complaint categories; service columns used for validation, not as topic inputs.
